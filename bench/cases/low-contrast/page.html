Keeping a sourdough starter alive

March 3 · 6 min read

A starter is a small ecosystem of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria, and like any ecosystem it wants a steady routine more than it wants attention. Feed it at the same time each day and it will reward you.

Use equal weights of flour and water. Whole rye flour gives the microbes more to eat and makes a lively starter faster than white flour does.

If you see a grey liquid on top, the starter is hungry, not dead. Pour it off, feed it, and keep it a little warmer for a day or two.

Once it doubles reliably within six hours of a feed, it is ready to raise bread.
